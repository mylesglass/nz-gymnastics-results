# Feature Requests
Ideas that my testers & I have dreamt of and would like to see implemented

- ✅ **Add overs/unders to the Nationals ranking** — done: the national + apparatus ranking pages have a WAG Division filter (All/Over/Under) that recomputes the ranking server-side.
- ✅ **AA ranking to app finals results** — done: all-around and apparatus results (with ranks) are shown in the wide tables and tooltips, and the season timeline + event pages expose per-apparatus/AA results.
- 🔲 **Teams results** — still open: club/region "team" results aren't aggregated into a separate team view (clubs are grouped by region on the /clubs page).
- ✅ **Madison Lynch comes up as both my Step 7 as well as an Omni Step 2 - with the same GNZ number...** — resolved: the athlete identity layer splits same-name/different-club athletes (the two Madison Lynches are separate profiles), and the admin Identity Review surfaces any remaining same-ID-different-people cases for manual merge/split.
- 🔲 **How many gymnasts were in each competition alongside overall ranking** — still open: event pages show total gymnast counts, but a per-competition "N out of M" alongside rank isn't shown.
- ✅ **Pull out each gymnast's best score on each apparatus and add them together** — done: the gymnast Personal Bests card shows the best per-apparatus score that season plus a "Best Possible AA" (sum of the apparatus bests) with an explanatory tooltip.
